SuperC gives every source line the #if condition under which it is compiled. A condition is in the build when the coverage build instrumented at least one of its lines, and covered when a trial executed one. Unconditional code (condition 1) and code no configuration compiles (0) are left out. An arm's trials are combined two ways: the union (what any of its trials covered) and the mean over its trials (± standard deviation: what a typical trial covers); every trial counts the conditions all of them covered.
